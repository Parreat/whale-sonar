              Settings: thresholds, watchlist, extra coins

              Every 15 seconds the site checks 5-minute trading volume on every meme pool it tracks (from DexScreener), then pulls whale swaps from the pools where the most money is moving (from GeckoTerminal, which allows about 10 requests a minute).
