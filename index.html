            Settings: thresholds, watchlist, extra coins

            Every 15 seconds the site checks 5-minute trading volume on every meme pool it tracks (from DexScreener), then pulls whale swaps from the pools where the most money is moving (from GeckoTerminal, which allows about 10 requests a minute). New pools come from GeckoTerminal's trending and new lists plus DexScreener's boosted and newly listed tokens; only pools with at least your minimum 24h volume and $10K of liquidity are tracked. Click any swap to open its details in a new tab. Stock prints, quotes, news, analyst ratings and insider filings come from Finnhub, and each print is tagged with the tick test because trades don't say who bought or sold. Extra Whale Sonar tabs mirror the first one instead of making their own requests. None of this is financial advice.
