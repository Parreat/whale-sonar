How the scores work

  • Stock signals on the dashboard blend analyst ratings (30%), the tone of the last 3 days of headlines by simple keyword matching (25%), large "block" trades on upticks versus downticks (25%), insider buying and selling over 30 days (10%), and today's price move (10%).
  • Stocks to watch in the newsletter use the same inputs without block trades: analyst ratings (35%), headline tone (35%), insider activity (15%) and the last session's move (15%). Stocks scoring +12 or more are listed for strength and −12 or less for weakness, out of a fixed list of about 40 large U.S. companies.
  • Top stories are ranked by how many market-moving words they contain and whether they name stocks on the list, and fade with age.
  • Whale trades are large on-chain swaps or stock prints over a size you choose. We can't know who is behind a trade or why, trades aren't labeled with a buyer or seller, and "buy" or "sell" on stock prints is a guess from the tick test.

These are simple heuristics. Keyword tone can misread headlines, analyst ratings lag, and data can be delayed, missing or wrong.
