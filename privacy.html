Privacy Policy

Last updated September 29, 2026

The short version. If you make an account we keep your email address, a scrambled (hashed) version of your password, and your newsletter choice. No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and we never sell or share your information. You can download or delete your data yourself at any time.

Who we are

Whale Sonar is run by the Whale Sonar team, which is responsible for your information (the "controller"). Contact us by opening an issue at github.com/Parreat/whale-sonar.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and your password, which we store only as a salted scrypt hash, so we can't see it.
  • Choices and consent records: whether you subscribed to the newsletter and when, whether you confirmed your email, which version of the Terms and this policy you accepted, and which newsletter issue we last sent you.
  • Sign-in sessions: a random session code in a cookie, plus when each session started and expires. We store only a hash of the code.
  • Ask usage: if you use Ask, how many questions you asked each day, so we can apply daily limits. Counts older than 7 days are deleted. We don't store the text of your questions, the details you enter about yourself, or the answers.
  • Security data: to slow down password guessing and spam sign-ups, the server counts recent requests by IP address in memory. These counts aren't written to disk and disappear within an hour.

We don't collect your name, phone number, location, payment details, or government ID. The optional "About you" details in Ask (amount to invest, time horizon, risk tolerance, experience, whether you have an emergency fund) are saved only in your browser and sent with each question, as described below; we don't keep them.

Ask and AI processing

When you ask a question in Ask, your question, the optional details you entered, and up to your last four questions and answers in the same conversation are sent to Anthropic, PBC, which runs the Claude AI model that writes the answer. To answer, the model may look up tickers you mention, and the server fetches that market data from Finnhub (only the ticker is sent). Anthropic processes this data as our service provider under its commercial terms and does not use it to train its models; it may keep it for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring. Please don't include sensitive personal information in your questions.

What stays in your browser

Your dashboard settings, watchlist and any API keys you enter (such as a Finnhub or CoinGecko key) are saved in your browser's local storage and are never sent to us. The dashboard loads market data directly from DexScreener, GeckoTerminal, CoinGecko and Finnhub, so those services receive your IP address and the requests your browser makes, under their own privacy policies.

Cookies

We use one strictly necessary cookie to keep you signed in. It's HttpOnly and not readable by scripts. We use no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking cookies or pixels, and our emails contain no tracking pixels or click tracking. Because the only cookie is strictly necessary, there's no cookie banner.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To run your account and sign you in: to perform our agreement with you.
  • To send Whale Sonar Daily: your consent, which you give by ticking the newsletter box and confirming your email, and can withdraw at any time.
  • To send account emails (confirmations, password resets, security notices): to perform our agreement, and our legitimate interest in keeping accounts secure.
  • To prevent abuse and keep the Service secure: our legitimate interest.
  • To keep records of consent and meet legal obligations, such as email marketing laws.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information and don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We use service providers that handle data only on our instructions: our hosting provider; our email delivery provider, which needs your email address to deliver messages; and, if you use Ask, Anthropic, as described above. We may disclose information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety or our rights, or to a successor if the Service is transferred, in which case this policy continues to apply.

How long we keep it

  • Accounts: until you delete them. Deleting removes your account, consent records and sessions from our database right away. Any backups roll off within 30 days.
  • Accounts whose email is never confirmed: deleted automatically after 7 days.
  • Sessions: expire after 30 days, or when you sign out or change your password.
  • Confirmation links expire after 48 hours and password reset links after 1 hour.

Your rights and choices

Wherever you live, you can do these yourself from the Account panel on the dashboard:

  • See and download your data: "Download my data" gives you everything we store about your account as a file.
  • Correct it: email us to change your email address.
  • Delete it: "Delete account" erases it.
  • Withdraw consent: unsubscribe using the link in any newsletter or the toggle in your account.

If you're in the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland, you also have the right to object to or restrict processing and to complain to your local data protection authority. If you're a California resident (or live in another U.S. state with a privacy law), you have the right to know, access, correct and delete your personal information, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We don't sell or share it, so there's nothing to opt out of, and we honor Global Privacy Control signals regardless. We won't treat you differently for using any of these rights. For anything you can't do yourself, contact us by opening an issue at github.com/Parreat/whale-sonar. We'll answer within 30 days, and may need to confirm the request comes from the account's email address.

Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt and a unique salt. Session and email codes are random, stored only as hashes, and expire. The site sends strict security headers, blocks cross-site requests, and limits repeated sign-in attempts. No system is perfectly secure, so use a password you don't use anywhere else. If a breach affects your information, we'll notify you and regulators as the law requires.

Children

The Service is for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has given us information, contact us by opening an issue at github.com/Parreat/whale-sonar and we'll delete it.

International transfers

Our servers and providers may be in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where the law requires, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Changes

We'll update the date above when this policy changes, and for material changes we'll email account holders before they take effect.
